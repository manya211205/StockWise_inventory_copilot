# Phase 3: Product Requirements and MVP Definition

## 1. Overview

**Product:** StockWise — Inventory Decision Copilot  
**Target user:** Inventory and operations managers at small D2C brands  
**Product type:** Rule-based inventory decision-support prototype

**Research status:** Product requirements and priorities are preliminary hypotheses. The target persona and problem have not yet been validated through interviews with real users.

## 2. Product Objective

Help inventory managers make informed replenishment decisions by identifying inventory risks, estimating order quantities, and understanding purchasing trade-offs under a limited budget.

**Core user decision:** Which products should I replenish, how much should I order, and what can I afford to purchase right now?

## 3. Initial Requirements

The proposed requirements are to identify inventory risks, recommend reorder quantities, explain recommendations, prioritize orders within a budget, and support inventory CSV uploads and exports.

These requirements must be validated against real user needs before they are treated as final.

## 4. MVP Scope

The proposed MVP includes inventory risk identification, reorder recommendations, a prioritized action list, budget-aware purchasing, and CSV input and export.

Demand forecasting, automatic supplier ordering, ERP integrations, and multi-user collaboration are outside the initial scope.

## 5. User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

The full user stories, acceptance criteria, provisional priorities, and success metrics will be documented as part of this phase.

## 6. Success Measurement

Potential measures include time to complete a replenishment review, recommendation usefulness, recommendation override rate, stockout rate, and inventory holding costs.

No improvement in these metrics has been established. Real-world measurement requires user testing and business data.

## 7. Open Questions

- Is budget-constrained replenishment a frequent and important user problem?
- Are the current risk classifications and reorder calculations suitable for real D2C workflows?
- Which requirements are essential to users?
- What evidence would demonstrate that StockWise creates measurable value?

## 8. Next Steps

Validate the problem and requirements through user interviews, refine the MVP based on evidence, and define a prioritization framework and experiment plan.

**Status:** Preliminary PRD and MVP scope; user validation pending.
